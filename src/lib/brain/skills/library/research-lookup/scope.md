# Research Lookup

Compile the external evidence needed to plan and justify a high-quality decision and report. The workflow targets **verified, unique sources** and produces an evidence basis (EVIDENCE lines + attached sources) rather than a loose list of links.

## Scope and boundaries

Use this skill when the work needs:

- background research for a decision, a design or the report
- several high-quality references (official docs, standards, peer-reviewed or authoritative sources)
- evidence supporting or contradicting a claim made in the Board
- a claim-to-source map (which EVIDENCE line supports which decision)
- current practice, precedent for a method, mechanisms, limitations, or gaps

Do not activate it for casual factual questions that do not need research, private or unpublished material, or a claim that can be answered from what Mkuu provided or from the Ledger.

This skill compiles **external evidence**. It cannot supply Mkuu's own private data, decide what his product's numbers show, or guarantee exhaustive completeness. For a formal systematic review, say so and scope it as its own agenda item.
