### 3. Select the governing standard

Identify the standard that governs the artifact: WCAG 2.2 for accessibility, OWASP ASVS / Top 10 for security, the relevant IETF RFC or HTTP semantics for APIs and protocols, official platform/framework guidelines, data-protection law where personal data is involved, and always the locked Ledger decisions. Use the current version and say which one.

**Critical distinction:** checklist completeness is not design quality, risk, validity, or merit. Never convert missing items into an automatic score or a verdict.
