### 7. Review ethics and integrity

Check applicable approvals, consent, welfare, privacy, community governance, funding, sponsor role, conflicts, authorship/contribution, registration, biosafety, and dual-use concerns.

Describe observable evidence and uncertainty. Do not accuse authors or investigate them. Raise credible concerns as a clear OBJECTION with the observable evidence; Mkuu decides.

For software this includes: authentication and authorization, secrets handling, personal data and consent, payments, dark patterns, and misuse potential — judged against the governing standard (OWASP, data-protection law).
